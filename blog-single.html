Finding the right light for a building

A useful starting point is to observe how daylight changes the character of a space before choosing a viewpoint.

Light influences the way we read a building: it can clarify a surface, soften a room or draw attention to a threshold. Before setting up, take time to see how it moves through the space and where its qualities best express the design.
